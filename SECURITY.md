# Security Policy

## Our Privacy Commitment

Second Soul is built on an unwavering principle: **Your data never leaves your phone.**

### Core Security Principles

🔒 **100% Local Processing**
- All behavioral data is collected and analyzed locally on your device
- Zero data transmission to external servers
- No cloud sync, no remote storage, no network transmission

🛡️ **No External Dependencies**
- We don't use third-party analytics services
- We don't send telemetry data
- We don't track you across platforms
- Your phone data remains exclusively yours

🔐 **Data Ownership**
- You own your digital soul
- You control who sees it
- You decide when and how to share
- You can delete everything anytime

## Reporting a Vulnerability

If you discover a security vulnerability in Second Soul, please report it **responsibly and privately**.

### How to Report

**Do NOT open a public GitHub issue for security vulnerabilities.**

Instead, please email: **yzan0250@gmail.com**

Include:
- Description of the vulnerability
- Steps to reproduce (if applicable)
- Potential impact
- Your name/contact info (optional)

### What to Expect

1. **Acknowledgment** - We'll confirm receipt within 48 hours
2. **Assessment** - We'll evaluate the severity and impact
3. **Update** - We'll work on a fix and notify you of progress
4. **Disclosure** - Once fixed, we'll release a patch and publicly disclose the issue (with credit if you wish)

## Security Best Practices for Users

While Second Soul handles security on our end, you can also protect yourself:

1. **Keep Your Device Secure**
   - Use strong passwords/biometrics
   - Keep OS updated
   - Use reputable antivirus software

2. **Review Permissions**
   - Understand what data Second Soul accesses
   - Only grant necessary permissions
   - Review app permissions regularly

3. **Backup Your Data**
   - While we store locally, device failures happen
   - Consider backing up your digital soul

4. **Be Cautious with Sharing**
   - Only share your digital soul with trusted people
   - Understand the implications of opening your profile
