## Principles We Won't Compromise

❌ **Never:** Collect data without explicit consent
❌ **Never:** Send data outside the user's device
❌ **Never:** Show inaccurate personality analysis as truth
❌ **Never:** Force social features
❌ **Never:** Sacrifice privacy for convenience

✅ **Always:** Prioritize user agency
✅ **Always:** Be transparent about limitations
✅ **Always:** Explain the psychology behind features
✅ **Always:** Make it easy to delete everything
✅ **Always:** Keep data local unless user chooses otherwise
