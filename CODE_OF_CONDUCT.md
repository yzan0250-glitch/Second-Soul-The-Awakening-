# Code of Conduct

## Our Commitment

Second Soul is built on the principle of **authenticity and honesty**. We are committed to providing a welcoming and inclusive environment for all contributors and community members, regardless of their background, experience level, or perspective.

We believe in the power of genuine connection - both between humans and within our code. This Code of Conduct reflects that commitment.

## Our Values

### 🎯 Authenticity
Be genuine. Share your real thoughts and concerns. We value honest criticism and open dialogue.

### 🔒 Respect for Privacy
We build this project for people seeking authentic connection while protecting their privacy. Respect others' data, boundaries, and choices about what they share.

### 🤝 Inclusivity
Everyone deserves to be heard. We welcome diverse perspectives, experiences, and approaches to solving problems.

### 🧠 Growth Mindset
We're all learning. If you make mistakes, we'll help you grow. If you see others struggling, offer support.

### 💡 Intellectual Honesty
Acknowledge limitations. Admit when you don't know something. Don't pretend certainty where there is doubt.

## Expected Behavior

- **Be Respectful** - Treat others with kindness and dignity
- **Be Constructive** - Offer solutions, not just criticism
- **Be Curious** - Ask questions to understand before judging
- **Be Transparent** - Be clear about your intentions and conflicts of interest
- **Be Patient** - Remember we're all busy; responses may take time
- **Be Supportive** - Help newcomers and celebrate others' achievements

## Unacceptable Behavior

We do not tolerate:
- Harassment, discrimination, or hate speech of any kind
- Personal attacks or insults
- Trolling or intentionally disruptive behavior
- Sharing others' private information without consent
- Spam or excessive self-promotion
- Any form of discrimination based on identity, background, or beliefs

## If You Witness or Experience Inappropriate Behavior

1. **Document it** - Keep a record of what happened, when, and who was involved
2. **Report it** - Contact the maintainers directly at yzan0250@gmail.com
3. **Be Specific** - Include details to help us understand and respond appropriately

## Our Responsibility

Project maintainers are responsible for:
- Clarifying what is expected behavior
- Enforcing this Code of Conduct fairly and consistently
- Addressing reported issues promptly and respectfully
- Protecting the reporter's privacy

We may remove contributions, ban users, or take other actions we deem appropriate in response to violations.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to:
- All project spaces (repository, issues, pull requests, discussions)
- Community interactions related to Second Soul
- Representations of the project in public spaces

## Final Note

**"Traditional platforms make you perform; Second Soul makes you exist."**

The same principle applies here. Be yourself. Be honest. Be kind. Together, we're building something authentic.
